# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

This policy explains which personal data Notebook (`notebook.dcdavidev.me`) processes, why, and what your rights are. Notebook is a free, non-commercial site with programming courses. It has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and no advertising.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Davide Di Criscito**, the author of Notebook. To contact him about your data, use the contact details on [dcdavidev.me](https://dcdavidev.me). Don't open a public GitHub issue for privacy requests.

## What data is processed

### Server logs

The site is hosted by **Vercel Inc.** When you open a page, your browser sends technical data that the hosting provider records: your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, and the type of browser and device.

- **Why:** to deliver the pages, keep the site secure, and find errors.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running a working, secure site.
- **How long:** for the short period set by Vercel's log retention.

### Your preferences

Notebook remembers your language, theme, palette, and cookie choice with technical cookies and your browser's local storage. These values stay on your device and contain no data about who you are. The [Cookie Policy](./cookies) lists each of them.

- **Legal basis:** they're strictly necessary to provide the site you asked for, so they don't need consent.

### Anonymous statistics (only with your consent)

If you accept the **Analytics** category in the cookie banner, the site loads **Vercel Web Analytics**. It records the pages you view, the page you came from, your country, and the type of browser, operating system, and device.

- Vercel Web Analytics doesn't use cookies. It recognizes a visit with a hash of the request that changes every day, so it can't follow you across days or across sites.
- **Why:** to see which courses people read and what to improve.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time from **Settings → Cookie preferences**. Withdrawing doesn't affect the processing done before.

## Who receives the data

- **Vercel Inc.**, as hosting and analytics provider, processes the data on behalf of the controller.
- Nobody else. The data isn't sold or shared for advertising.

Vercel is based in the United States, so data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. These transfers rely on the safeguards that Vercel provides, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Links to other sites

The site links to GitHub, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, thanks.dev, and other sites. When you follow a link, the other site's privacy policy applies.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your data and get a copy of it;
- have it corrected or deleted;
- restrict or object to its processing;
- receive it in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise them, use the contact details above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy it's the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).

## Changes

This policy may change when the site changes. The date at the top shows the last update.
