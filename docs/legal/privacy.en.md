# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

This policy explains which personal data Notebook (`notebook.dcdavidev.me`) processes, why, and what your rights are. Notebook is a free, non-commercial site with programming courses. You can read every course without an account. If you want one, you can sign in with Google, GitHub, or Facebook. There's no advertising.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Davide Di Criscito**, the author of Notebook. To contact him about your data, use the contact details on [dcdavidev.me](https://dcdavidev.me). Don't open a public GitHub issue for privacy requests.

## What data is processed

### Server logs

The site is hosted by **Vercel Inc.** When you open a page, your browser sends technical data that the hosting provider records: your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, and the type of browser and device.

- **Why:** to deliver the pages, keep the site secure, and find errors.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running a working, secure site.
- **How long:** for the short period set by Vercel's log retention.

### Your preferences

Notebook remembers your language, theme, palette, and cookie choice with technical cookies and your browser's local storage. These values stay on your device and contain no data about who you are. If you sign in, your language, theme, and palette are also saved in your account (see below). The [Cookie Policy](./cookies) lists each of them.

- **Legal basis:** they're strictly necessary to provide the site you asked for, so they don't need consent.

### Your account (only if you sign in)

You can sign in with a **Google**, **GitHub**, or **Facebook** account. There's no sign-in with an email and password. When you sign in, the provider you choose sends Notebook your name, email address, and profile picture. Notebook saves them together with an identifier of your account. You can change the name shown on the site on your [account page](/account).

- **Why:** to create your account, show who is signed in, and keep your language, theme, and palette on every device. Later, also to save your favorites and progress.
- **What else:** the language, theme, and palette you choose, and the other accounts (Google, GitHub, Facebook) you link to your account.
- **Legal basis:** providing the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **How long:** until you delete your account. On your [account page](/account), you can download a copy of your data and delete your account with all its data. You can also use the contact details above.
- You sign in on the provider's site, under its own privacy policy. Notebook never sees your password.
- Your profile picture is loaded from the provider's servers.

### Anonymous statistics (only with your consent)

If you accept the **Analytics** category in the cookie banner, the site loads **Vercel Web Analytics**. It records the pages you view, the page you came from, your country, and the type of browser, operating system, and device.

- Vercel Web Analytics doesn't use cookies. It recognizes a visit with a hash of the request that changes every day, so it can't follow you across days or across sites.
- **Why:** to see which courses people read and what to improve.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time from **Settings → Cookie preferences**. Withdrawing doesn't affect the processing done before.

## Who receives the data

- **Vercel Inc.**, as hosting and analytics provider, processes the data on behalf of the controller.
- **Supabase Inc.**, as the provider of accounts and their database, processes account data on behalf of the controller.
- **Google**, **GitHub**, or **Meta (Facebook)**: only the one you sign in with.
- Nobody else. The data isn't sold or shared for advertising.

Vercel and Supabase are based in the United States, so data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. These transfers rely on the safeguards that these providers offer, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Links to other sites

The site links to GitHub, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, thanks.dev, and other sites. When you follow a link, the other site's privacy policy applies.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your data and get a copy of it;
- have it corrected or deleted;
- restrict or object to its processing;
- receive it in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise them, use the contact details above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy it's the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).

## Changes

This policy may change when the site changes. The date at the top shows the last update.
