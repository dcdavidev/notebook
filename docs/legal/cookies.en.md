# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Cookies are small text files that a site stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar space in the browser where a site can keep data. This policy lists what Notebook stores, and how you can change your choice. The [Privacy Policy](./privacy) explains how personal data is processed.

## Necessary

They make the site work and remember your choices. They're always on. Only the sign-in cookies identify you, and only after you sign in.

| Name                                                          | Type          | Purpose                                     | Duration           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| `nb-cookie-consent`                                           | Cookie        | Remembers your choice in the cookie banner  | 1 year             |
| `nb-locale`                                                   | Cookie        | Remembers the language you chose            | 1 year             |
| `sb-…-auth-token`, `sb-…-auth-token-code-verifier`            | Cookie        | Keep you signed in (only after you sign in) | Until you sign out |
| `nb-palette`                                                  | Local storage | Remembers the color palette                 | Until you clear it |
| `mui-mode`, `mui-color-scheme-light`, `mui-color-scheme-dark` | Local storage | Remember the light or dark theme            | Until you clear it |

All of them are first-party: only Notebook can read them.

## Analytics

Off until you accept them.

If you accept this category, the site loads **Vercel Web Analytics**, which counts page views anonymously. It **doesn't store cookies** or other data in your browser: it recognizes a visit with a hash of the request that changes every day. The [Privacy Policy](./privacy) describes the data it collects.

## External content

Off until you accept it.

This category covers images, videos, and other content loaded from external sites, which can set their own cookies. **At the moment Notebook doesn't embed any external content.** If a page adds some in the future, it will load only if you accept this category.

## How to change your choice

- On the site: open **Settings → Cookie preferences** and switch the categories on or off.
- In your browser: you can delete cookies and local storage, or block them, in the browser settings. If you delete them, the site forgets your preferences and shows the banner again.

## Changes

This policy may change when the site changes. The date at the top shows the last update.
