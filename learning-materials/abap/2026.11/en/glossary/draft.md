## Draft

In RAP, a working copy of the data that the user edits and saves later, without losing it when closing the app. Lesson [146](../course/0146-rap-draft.md).

## ETag

In RAP, a field (usually the last-changed time stamp) that the service compares so it does not overwrite another user's changes. Lessons [140](../course/0140-rap-data-model.md), [146](../course/0146-rap-draft.md).
