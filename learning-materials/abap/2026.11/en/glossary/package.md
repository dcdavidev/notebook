## Package

The container that groups development objects and sets their transport and visibility. Lesson [7](../course/0007-packages-and-transport-requests.md).

## Customer namespace

Names that start with `Z` or `Y` (or with a registered prefix such as `/ABC/`), reserved for customer objects so they never clash with SAP's. Lesson [7](../course/0007-packages-and-transport-requests.md).
