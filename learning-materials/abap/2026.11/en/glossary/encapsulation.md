## Encapsulation

Hiding a class's internal details in `PRIVATE` and `PROTECTED` sections and exposing only a `PUBLIC` interface. Lesson [80](../course/0080-visibility-and-encapsulation.md).

## Visibility

The `PUBLIC`, `PROTECTED`, and `PRIVATE` sections of a class, which decide who can use each component. Lesson [80](../course/0080-visibility-and-encapsulation.md).
