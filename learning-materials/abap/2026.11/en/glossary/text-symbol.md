## Text symbol

A program text (`TEXT-001`) stored separately and translatable into other languages. Lesson [62](../course/0062-text-symbols-and-translation.md).

## Text pool

The set of a program's texts (text symbols, titles, selection texts), translated into other languages with SE63. Lesson [62](../course/0062-text-symbols-and-translation.md).
