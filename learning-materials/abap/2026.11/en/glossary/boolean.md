## `abap_bool`, `abap_true`, `abap_false`

The type and the constants for Boolean values: in ABAP true is `'X'` and false is a blank. Lesson [16](../course/0016-boolean-values.md).

## `xsdbool`

The function that turns a logical expression into `abap_true` or `abap_false`. Lesson [16](../course/0016-boolean-values.md).
