## Primary key

The set of fields that uniquely identifies a row, in a database table or an internal table. Lessons [35](../course/0035-table-keys.md), [46](../course/0046-database-tables.md).

## Secondary key

An extra sorted or hashed key of an internal table, for fast reads by criteria other than the primary key. Lesson [35](../course/0035-table-keys.md).
