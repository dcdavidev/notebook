## ALV (ABAP List Viewer)

The standard tool for displaying data tables with sorting, filters, totals, and export; used through `CL_SALV_TABLE` or `CL_GUI_ALV_GRID`. Lessons [99](../course/0099-alv-with-salv.md), [100](../course/0100-alv-grid-control.md).

## CL_SALV_TABLE

The class that builds a complete ALV list in a few lines from an internal table, with the standard functions ready to use. Lesson [99](../course/0099-alv-with-salv.md).

## CL_GUI_ALV_GRID

The classic ALV control, placed on a Dynpro, that supports editable cells, events, and a custom field catalog. Lesson [100](../course/0100-alv-grid-control.md).

## Field catalog

The table that describes the columns of an ALV: field, label, width, visibility, editability. Lesson [100](../course/0100-alv-grid-control.md).
