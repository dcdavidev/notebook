## Selection screen

A report's input screen, built with `PARAMETERS`, `SELECT-OPTIONS`, and `SELECTION-SCREEN`. Lessons [63](../course/0063-parameters.md), [65](../course/0065-selection-screen-layout.md).

## `PARAMETERS`

The statement that creates a single input field on a report's selection screen. Lesson [63](../course/0063-parameters.md).

## `SELECT-OPTIONS`

The statement that creates a selection screen field for intervals and lists of values, backed by a range table. Lesson [64](../course/0064-select-options-and-ranges.md).

## Variant

A saved set of values for a selection screen, to reuse or to use in a background job. Lesson [68](../course/0068-variants-and-background-jobs.md).
