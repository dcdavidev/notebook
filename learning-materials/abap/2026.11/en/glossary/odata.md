## OData

The REST protocol that SAP Fiori apps use to read and write data; in ABAP it is exposed with SAP Gateway or with RAP. Lessons [128](../course/0128-odata-with-sap-gateway.md), [144](../course/0144-rap-business-services.md).

## SAP Gateway

The component that exposes ABAP data and logic as OData services (transaction SEGW). Lesson [128](../course/0128-odata-with-sap-gateway.md).
