## Inheritance

The mechanism by which a subclass receives attributes and methods from a superclass (`INHERITING FROM`) and can redefine them (`REDEFINITION`). Lesson [82](../course/0082-inheritance.md).

## Superclass, subclass

In inheritance, the superclass is the class inherited from and the subclass is the one that inherits, with `INHERITING FROM`. Lesson [82](../course/0082-inheritance.md).

## `REDEFINITION`

The addition that rewrites an inherited method in a subclass; `super->` still calls the superclass version. Lesson [82](../course/0082-inheritance.md).
