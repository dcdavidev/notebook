## Polymorphism

The ability to call the same method on objects of different classes and get each class's own behavior. Lesson [85](../course/0085-polymorphism-and-casting.md).

## Casting

Assigning a reference to a variable of another type: _upcast_ towards the superclass, _downcast_ (`CAST`, `?=`) towards the subclass. Lesson [85](../course/0085-polymorphism-and-casting.md).
