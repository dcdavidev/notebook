## IDoc (Intermediate Document)

SAP's standard format for exchanging business documents between systems, used with ALE and EDI. Lesson [125](../course/0125-idoc-and-ale.md).

## ALE (Application Link Enabling)

The technology for asynchronous data exchange between SAP systems, usually through IDocs. Lesson [125](../course/0125-idoc-and-ale.md).
