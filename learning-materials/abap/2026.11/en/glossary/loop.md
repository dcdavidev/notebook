## Loop

A block of code that repeats: `DO`, `WHILE`, or `LOOP AT` over the rows of an internal table. Lessons [26](../course/0026-do-and-while-loops.md), [38](../course/0038-looping-internal-tables.md).

## `EXIT`, `CONTINUE`

The statements that, in a loop, respectively leave the loop and skip to the next pass. Lesson [27](../course/0027-exit-continue-check.md).

## `CHECK`

The statement that leaves the current block, or skips to the next loop pass, if the condition is false. Lesson [27](../course/0027-exit-continue-check.md).
