## ABAP Unit

The framework for automated unit tests: local test classes marked `FOR TESTING` and assertions from `CL_ABAP_UNIT_ASSERT`. Lesson [105](../course/0105-abap-unit-basics.md).

## Test double

A fake object that replaces a real dependency (database, API) during a test. Lesson [106](../course/0106-test-doubles.md).
