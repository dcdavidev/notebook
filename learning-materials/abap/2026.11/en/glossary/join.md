## Join

Combining rows from several related tables in a single `SELECT`: `INNER JOIN`, `LEFT OUTER JOIN`, and others. Lesson [55](../course/0055-joins.md).

## `INNER JOIN`, `LEFT OUTER JOIN`

The two most common joins: an inner join keeps only rows with a match in every table, a left outer join keeps every row of the left table. Lesson [55](../course/0055-joins.md).
