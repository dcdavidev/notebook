## Method

A procedure that belongs to a class and works on its attributes; declared with `METHODS` and implemented between `METHOD` and `ENDMETHOD`. Lesson [77](../course/0077-attributes-and-methods.md).

## Method parameter

A value passed to or from a method: `IMPORTING`, `EXPORTING`, `CHANGING`, or `RETURNING`. Lesson [78](../course/0078-method-parameters.md).
