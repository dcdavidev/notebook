## Smart Forms

The tool for designing print forms (invoices, orders) with layout, texts, and data, which generates a function module to call. Lesson [129](../course/0129-forms.md).

## Adobe Forms

Print and interactive forms in PDF format, designed with Adobe LiveCycle Designer and called from ABAP. Lesson [129](../course/0129-forms.md).
