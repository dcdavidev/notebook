## Regular expression

A text pattern used with `FIND`, `REPLACE`, and `matches` to search and replace parts of strings. Lesson [96](../course/0096-regular-expressions.md).

## `FIND`, `REPLACE`

The statements that search and replace text in a string, with fixed text or a regular expression (`PCRE`). Lesson [96](../course/0096-regular-expressions.md).
