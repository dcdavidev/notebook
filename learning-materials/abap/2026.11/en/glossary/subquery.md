## Subquery

A `SELECT` inside another `SELECT`, used for example with `IN` or `EXISTS` in the `WHERE` condition. Lesson [58](../course/0058-subqueries-and-union.md).

## `UNION`

The SQL operator that merges the rows of two `SELECT` statements into one result. Lesson [58](../course/0058-subqueries-and-union.md).
