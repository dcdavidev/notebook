## Report

An executable program (`REPORT`), usually with a selection screen and a list or ALV output. Lessons [8](../course/0008-hello-world.md), [66](../course/0066-report-events.md).

## Report event

A block the runtime executes at a precise moment of a report: `INITIALIZATION`, `AT SELECTION-SCREEN`, `START-OF-SELECTION`, and others. Lesson [66](../course/0066-report-events.md).
