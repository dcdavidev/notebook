## Enhancement

An extension of SAP standard code without modifying it: implicit (at the start or end of blocks) or explicit (enhancement points and sections). Lessons [120](../course/0120-extending-standard-sap.md), [123](../course/0123-enhancement-points.md).

## Modification

A direct change to SAP standard code, recorded and to be reviewed at every upgrade: used only when no extension exists. Lesson [120](../course/0120-extending-standard-sap.md).
