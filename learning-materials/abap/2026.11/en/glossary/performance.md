## SQL trace (ST05)

The tool that records every SQL statement of a program with its duration and row count, to find slow reads. Lesson [108](../course/0108-performance-analysis.md).

## ABAP profiler (SAT)

The tool that measures the time spent in each part of a program, in ABAP and in the database. Lesson [108](../course/0108-performance-analysis.md).
